# POSTURE

M1 asks three questions. An answer here is a rule, or it is not governance.

## What may change
Everything inside 0x1/. Nothing outside that boundary is ours to change.

## Who may change it
Anyone who can show the evidence for the change. Standing here is a demonstrated result, not a title.

## How change is proven
Every change arrives with the claim, the command, and the output it printed. A commit message asserting a result without the output behind it is a claim. A claim is not a change.

## What is not evidence
- A summary of what a tool did, written by that tool
- "Complete" or "correct" with no run behind it
- A version number asserting a property
- The plan for the commands, offered in place of their output
- A result that does not reproduce from the same inputs

## When a change is found wrong
Revert first, discuss second. A wrong claim left standing costs more than a reverted commit.
